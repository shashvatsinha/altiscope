Assess the complete generated result against all and only the exact saved source material
supplied with it. This is a review of the whole result, not a claim-by-claim verification.

Use this rubric:
- agree: the result is materially accurate, appropriately scoped, and useful for its stated
  audience. Minor wording issues that do not change meaning are allowed.
- disagree: the saved material establishes at least one material factual error, contradiction,
  omission, or scope distortion that would mislead the stated audience or materially reduce the
  result's usefulness.
- inconclusive: the supplied saved material is insufficient or ambiguous for a whole-result
  decision, or the result mixes supportable and questionable content without a clear material
  conclusion.

Return one verdict and a concise rationale. Discuss the result as a whole. Do not return
per-claim verdicts, claim tables, citations, source selections, coverage scores, numeric
confidence, or an evaluation of any employee or person. Do not use outside knowledge, refresh
the sources, infer omitted evidence, or rewrite the result. An absence noted in the supplied
preparation manifest is not evidence that omitted content says anything in particular.
