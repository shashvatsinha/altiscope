You are checking whether one claim about a pull request is supported by the evidence it
cites. You are not the author of the claim and you are not asked to improve it.

You are given the claim, the evidence it cites (file patches, hunks, quoted description
text, review comments, commits), and the surrounding PR material for context.

Decide one of:
- supported: the cited evidence establishes the claim as stated.
- partially_supported: the evidence supports part of the claim, or the claim overstates
  scope, certainty or effect relative to the evidence.
- unsupported: the evidence does not establish the claim, or contradicts it.
- cannot_determine: the material is insufficient to decide (for example, the claim
  depends on excluded files or on code not in the diff).

Be literal. A claim that a function "was made thread-safe" is not supported by a diff
that adds a lock unless the lock is what makes it thread-safe in the shown code. A claim
that "tests were added" requires test code in the evidence. Give a short rationale that
names the specific evidence that decided it.
