Billing retry, deduplication, and failure diagnostics

Billing processing adds bounded retries for temporary failures and transactional event-ID deduplication at intake. Failure counters, job-ID logs, and focused tests support investigation of those paths.

Processing behavior
Temporary charge failures are requeued up to the retry limit. Event intake stores an ID and enqueues within one transaction; a repeated ID returns before enqueueing. Neither change establishes exactly-once external charging.

Diagnostics and tests
Failed jobs increment a counter and log their job ID. Tests check the retry limit and duplicate-event behavior. Alert thresholds and production reliability measurements are not included.
